Not everything worth drinking is sweet. Tikt is the aperitivo hour, uncorked from the bottle and slipped into a can.

Tikt (noun): bitter, in Sanskrit

The Americano

Three ingredients.
Centuries of tradition.

Soda Water

The crisp backbone. Bright, effervescent, clean.

Campari

The soul. Bitter orange, gentian root, aromatic complexity.

Sweet Vermouth

The warmth. Botanicals, caramel depth, a velvet finish.

330ML  |  5% ABV  |  All Natural  |  Handcrafted in Chicago

The Ritual

01

Chill

Straight from the fridge. Tikt is best at 38–42°F.

02

Pour (or don't)

Over ice in a rocks glass, or sip it straight from the can. No judgment.

03

Garnish

A dried orange peel. A Castelvetrano olive. A twist of grapefruit. Make it yours.
